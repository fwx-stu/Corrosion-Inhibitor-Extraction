# Balanced Policy
- Recover all clearly explicit experimental and computational source anchors while rejecting inferred combinations.
- Prefer table row/column alignment over narrative reconstruction.
- Emit explicit condition records even when optional fields are null.
- Populate a field only when it is explicitly linked to the same inhibitor and source anchor.
- Split genuine multi-condition tables, but never expand numeric ranges or combine independent lists.
- Deduplicate overlapping chunks, repeated tables, and narrative restatements.
