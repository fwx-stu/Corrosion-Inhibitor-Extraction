# Precision-Oriented Policy
- Preserve every explicit source anchor, but populate each field only when its linkage to that record is explicit.
- Use null rather than transferring a condition or value from a nearby but unaligned row, table, inhibitor, phase, or method.
- Do not infer concentrations, temperatures, times, methods, or efficiencies from common practice.
- Do not use HOMO, LUMO, adsorption energy, binding energy, or other field labels as test_method.
- Keep delta_g_ads and adsorption_energy distinct.
- Exclude synthesis-only, characterization-only, background, and reference material that does not define a valid target record.
- Never create a Cartesian product or enumerate a range.
